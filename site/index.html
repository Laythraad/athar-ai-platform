Trust & method

What is checked, what is approximate, and what is machine translated.

Coordinate precision

Verified

Coordinates checked against the monument or the property boundary.

On site

Coordinates point at the site itself, accurate to a few tens of metres.

City centre

Coordinates point at the city or town; the settlement is certain, the exact spot is not.

Sources

Every place lists the reference it was built from — UNESCO, official institutions or a recognised reference work.

Method

  1. Places are selected for global coverage across six continents, then normalised into one schema.
  2. Coordinates are stored as WGS84 decimal degrees and labelled with their precision.
  3. Interface text is written in English and Arabic; the other 16 core languages are translations. Any other language is machine translated and labelled.

About the AI guide

The guide receives the place records shown in this app plus your question. It does not have access to anything else about you.

Source links and coordinates were last checked in October 2026.
